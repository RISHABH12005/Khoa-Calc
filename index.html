Common Hindi market words:

  • पाव (Paav) — ¼ kilogram = 250 grams
  • आधा किलो (Aadha kilo) — ½ kilogram = 500 grams
  • तीन पाव (Teen paav) — ¾ kilogram = 750 grams
  • एक किलो (Ek kilo) — 1 kilogram = 1000 grams

Example: “ढाई सौ ग्राम” (Dhai sau gram) means 250 grams.
